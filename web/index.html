      How to read the evidence

      Archive captures

      Wayback’s index reports a capture timestamp and recorded status. Replay can fail or show missing assets. A gap between captures tells you about coverage.

      Current URL checks

      Redirects and HTTP statuses are observations from one network at a stated time. They can vary by country, protocol, or visit.

      Replacement pages

      Official successor pages are research leads. A department homepage may cover the same service without preserving the old page’s text.
